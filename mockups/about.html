Module 00 / About

A calmer way to decide.

[Placeholder] Pause is a personal tool that reads your own order history and shows you what you already own before you buy something new — what it costs this month, and whether it's worth the wait.

Why it exists

[Placeholder] One-click checkouts remove the gap between wanting something and living with it. Pause restores that gap, gently — never by blocking a purchase, only by slowing the moment down.

How it works

[Placeholder] Connect Gmail once. Pause reads order-confirmation emails to build your wardrobe automatically, then compares anything new you're considering against what you already own.

Privacy

[Placeholder] Read-only access, always. Nothing is sent, deleted, or modified in your inbox, and nothing is shared. Disconnect or permanently delete your data at any time.
